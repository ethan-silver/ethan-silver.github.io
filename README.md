I'm a fourth year PhD candidate in the Dept. of Physics at Harvard, advised by Edo Berger in the Dept. of Astronomy. My current research involves using time-domain surveys such as ZTF and Vera Rubin Observatory's LSST to search for and study supernova precursors, the enhanced emission and brightening that can be observed months to years before a massive star explodes as a supernova. Studying the frequency and properties of supernova precursors can help us better understand the underlying mechanisms of the stellar instability and mass loss that occurs before explosion, and ultimately advance our understanding of the final stages of massive star evolution as well as binary star evolution.
